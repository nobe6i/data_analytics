BAN 612 Week 5 practice dataset: SaaS customer revenue and engagement

Business context: A B2B SaaS company wants to create a customer-month feature table for revenue, usage, and support-ticket analysis.

Files:
- customers.csv: one row per customer account.
- subscriptions.csv: subscription periods and plan information. A few customers upgrade plans.
- invoices.csv: invoice-level revenue and payment behavior.
- weekly_usage.csv: weekly product usage metrics.
- support_tickets.csv: support interactions and customer satisfaction.
- usage_wide_demo.csv: deliberately wide monthly usage table for reshape practice.

The dataset is designed for data wrangling practice, not as a cleaning-only exercise. Students should build transformed features at appropriate units of analysis and document assumptions.
